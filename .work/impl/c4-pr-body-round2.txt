After the merge, a tui4s app has components in two styles. Hooks (`tui4s.Hooks`): a component is a function with `useSignal`, `useHold`, `useFold`, `useEffect` and `useInterval`, and `component(key)(body)` is a node that the runner mounts (the body runs at the mount, in a `Scope` that closes at the unmount). The class style (`tui4s.Component`): a `State` type member, `initial`, `view(state)` and `onMount(state)`; the runner keeps the state under the position and the overridable `stateKey` (the class name by default). ## Problems and decisions in this PR

- The eleventh to thirteenth rounds are not in the local `.work/DECISIONS.md`; they are read from `origin/wip/handoff` (7a4e449).
- The commit trailer is "Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5": the attribution of the session (as in C3 and T1); the task text named "Claude Fable 5.1".
- Step 3 comes before 4a: the carried main code compiles with only the strict-flag fix; the test sources need 4a.
- The restored prototype code had five bugs (P-6). The fixes change the component rules of the prototype in two places: a `Component` mount goes on while the same state key stays at the same position (the prototype restarted `onMount` for each new instance), and only the frame of a component's mount waits for its first content, at most `pendingTimeout` in all (the prototype waited per message and again at each render). AGENTS.md and the Scaladoc describe the new rules. See "Questions for the user".
- The plan asked for "R1 at Mount.scala:128, 147" (the two fibers that the mount starts). The fibers that the hooks start (`useEffect`, `useInterval`, `useHold`, `useFold`) are not started by `Mount`; they join the gauge through the new Local `internal.Tui4sTrack` (as `Tui4sQuit` carries the quit). Without it, a hook at the top of the app (its `Scope` is the kit's, Kyo RC7 `Fiber.init`) runs on after the run until the kit's scope ends (negative control A).
- The pending wait of the runner converts `pendingTimeout` to nanoseconds (a negative value is 0, a value too long for nanoseconds is `Long.MaxValue`) and builds the Kyo duration with `Duration.fromNanos`, not `Duration.fromJava`: one deadline per frame needs nanoseconds.
- `armkey` and the verify pass do not change: a component region is not young and the verify pass skips it (`verifyRegion` has no case for `Source.Comp`); the regions inside its content are young as any mounted region.
- A `Component` inside a region whose UI holds closures (a `when`, a `render`): the verify pass and the repair tick (1 s) render the parent again (gate 1 item 3), so `view` of a new instance runs again and the regions inside its view get new watchers; the mount (state, `onMount`) goes on. No cells change, and the terminal gets no bytes.
- Without a key, `component(body)` keeps its first mount when its parent renders another value at that position (the identity is the call site): this is the prototype's rule, written in the Scaladoc of `Hooks.component` and pinned by a HooksTest leaf. Give a key (`component(user.id)(...)`) for a fresh mount.
- A hook component's body or an `onMount` that fails is silent (P-12; "Questions for the user").
- `tui4s.internal.Hooks` (the shutdown hooks of T1) and the public `tui4s.Hooks` share a simple name. `Tui.scala` and the test double `FakeCrossterm.scala` import the internal one by name (an explicit import wins over a member of the package from another file); NameClashTest compiles with `import tui4s.Hooks.*` and the wildcard imports of the public packages; the new suites import `tui4s.Hooks.*` and only the internal names that they use.
